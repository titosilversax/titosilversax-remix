SESSION NOTES

If music has ever felt like the only thing that understood you

You're in the right place.

I'm Tito — a Puerto Rican-American ambient saxophonist and state-certified peer support specialist in Cleveland, Ohio. Drawing on mystical experience, contemplative Christian faith, and Taíno roots, I play spirit-first, swimming in what Toru Takemitsu called the sea of tonality: improvisation over my own arrangements, with advanced harmony as the poetic language. I wrote The Saxophonist's Compendium and The Emotional Star Map, and I play out in Cleveland's jazz and improv scene. Sam Newsome, Takemitsu, Debussy, Satie, Beethoven, Tomasz Stańko, and Stephan Micus shape my ear; Coltrane and Miles Davis remain my muses.

Peer Support · Modal Music — every recording and every guide comes from the same practice, not a course written about it.
